SableEdit demo

Click any shape to edit it. Click empty space to deselect. Every shape here is a live element; edits write straight to its attributes.
nothing selected
  • Path: drag square nodes and round bezier handles. Hold Shift on a cubic handle to mirror its partner. Double-click the path to add a node; double-click a node to delete it. Arcs have their own handles (below).
  • Arcs (A segments in a path): click an arc's end node or its dot to activate it and show its ellipse. The square on the x-axis sets rx and rotation; the square on the y-axis sets ry; hold Shift to keep it circular. Drag the round dot on the arc across the chord to flip the large-arc/sweep flags.
  • Rect: eight square handles resize (drag past the opposite edge to flip); the centre dot moves it.
  • Circle / ellipse: centre dot moves; square handles set the radius (rx/ry for the ellipse).
  • Line: drag either endpoint; the midpoint dot moves the whole line.
  • Polygon / polyline: drag vertex squares; the centre dot moves the whole shape. Double-click an edge to add a vertex, double-click a vertex to delete it (minimum 3 for a polygon, 2 for a polyline).
  • Undo/redo: Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z (or Ctrl+Y). A whole drag is one step.
